My Story

I first fell in love with design in school. I've always been more of a creative person and that was often expressed through art. That lead me to discover design!. That curiosity grew into a career, and today I help businesses and creatives turn their ideas into visuals that feel true to who they are. I'm currently studying at BCIT trying to level up my skills.

How I Work

Every project starts with listening. I take time to understand your goals, your audience, and what makes you different. From there, I move through sketching, exploring, and refining until the design feels right. I'm big on collaboration, clear communication, and delivering work on time without cutting corners.

What I Do

Brand identities and logo design
Print design (posters, packaging, menus, brochures)
Social media and digital graphics
Typography and layout

What I Believe In

Great design isn't just about looking good. It's about making a message clear and giving it personality. I love simple ideas, strong typography, and colour that means something.

Beyond the Screen

When I'm not designing, you'll usually find me sketching in a notebook or curled up with a good book. Sketching keeps my creativity flowing and helps me work through ideas without any pressure, while reading fuels my imagination and introduces me to new perspectives and stories. Both of these quiet habits shape the way I see the world, and you'll often find traces of them in my work.
